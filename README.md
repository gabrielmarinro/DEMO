# Demo Fleets Intelligence

## Turning fleet signals into decisions.

A fleet intelligence demo designed to move from **what happened → why it happened → what it could cost → what should happen next**.

The experience combines operational signals, predictive intelligence, business impact and recommended action inside a multi-role **Control Tower 360°**.

### What the demo explores

- **Predictive Maintenance** — prioritizes assets by cost of delay and surfaces intervention opportunities.
- **Compliance & Risk** — connects safety, documentation, inspections and regulatory alerts to immediate action.
- **Asset Lifecycle & Capital** — evaluates replacement decisions through ROI, accumulated cost, generated revenue and payback.
- **Fuel & Operating Cost** — detects consumption anomalies, benchmark deviation and potential savings.
- **Root-Cause Exploration** — moves from a signal or gap into the underlying drivers of the problem.
- **Executive Decision Support** — translates operational conditions into financial and strategic consequences.

### The interaction model

**Signal → finding → root cause → impact → action**

The interface is designed around a simple principle:

> **A dashboard describes the operation. Intelligence helps decide what to do next.**

### Control Tower 360°

The demo supports different operating perspectives, including:

**Monitorista · Supervisor · Jefe de Taller · Gerente de Flota · Director / CFO**

This allows the same operating state to be interpreted at different decision levels.

## Why this demo exists

The objective is to explore a different operating model for fleet software:

**from monitoring events to anticipating operational and financial consequences.**

The demo uses structured data and simulated operational scenarios to demonstrate the intelligence layer and interaction model.
